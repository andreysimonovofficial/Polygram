# DNA Code and Manifesto

**Language:** **English** | [Русский](../ru/01-strategy/vision-and-manifesto.md)

## DNA Code

Polygram in its essence is a clean CAD with API, MCP and other connectors. The system is modular. You can create absolutely any extensions. Everything connects maximally seamlessly. Whatever extensions you connect, it is still just data transfer. Request and result. Everything is simple. Polygram just makes all this visually ergonomic and performs calculations (coefficients, formulas, etc.).

## Maximum Leverage Search Philosophy

Principle: Thoughtful choice of one maximum leverage and several secondary ones based on deep analysis in the direction of scalable modules and primarily implementing the maximum leverage during the iteration, postponing secondary ones, then moving to the next iteration.

## Cosmopolitanism

Polygram positions itself as a tool for global use. Cosmopolitanism, aesthetics, visionary thinking — our references (Tesla, SpaceX).

## Founder's Principle

The product is made first of all for oneself as an ideal weapon, and users and investors are shown a ready-made working case. Transparency: Free free access to the basic vanilla CAD.

## Positioning

"Create. Manage. Reap." Enrichment tool and gamified "addictive" business activity that step by step leads to success.
