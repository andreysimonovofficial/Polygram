# ICP and Personas

**Language:** **English** | [Русский](../ru/04-marketing/icp-and-personas.md)

## First Buyer (Ideal Customer Profile)

For whom this problem hurts most right now.

New generation of entrepreneurs. People scaling Their comfort through assistants.

Startupers, entrepreneurs and project managers, Those Who need transparency and efficiency and minimal (ideally zero) attention fragmentation; And entrepreneurs Who cooperate with non-professional investors, Who do not understand documents, but want to see a simple explanation of what is happening, where They are investing money and understand everything in simple language, receiving the most up-to-date reports in an understandable format.

## Secondary avatar

Broad audience. For example, even a guy and a girl, Who are in a relationship can use Polygram in everyday life to achieve Their certain goal faster (For example, buy a house); And AI Agent orchestration is an additional leverage of efficiency, but must be performed perfectly so as not to burden users who use the application in everyday life.
