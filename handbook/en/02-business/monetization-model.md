# Monetization Model

**Language:** **English** | [Русский](../ru/02-business/monetization-model.md)

How Polygram makes money:

* Sale, rental, maintenance of AI servers;
* Sale of AI Corporation "out of the box";
* Personal technical support and expertise;
* CPA thanks to the desirable target audience for many advertisers within the platform;
* Cloud capacities;
* Cloud version (Premium) real-time interactive image streaming;
* Extensions in the application;
* Corporate sponsorship from industry giants;
* Community material support;
* Investment platform within Polygram. Direct investments into promising user AI Corporations;
* Royalties for commercial fork;
* In-platform currency Polys, which are AI credits, with which you can purchase goods from partners similar to Bitrefill, pay for premium functions, as well as commission for currency transfers between users;
* Live streams
  * Live stream with a live Polygram representative
    * Paid case review in live stream and providing a department (subgraph) as a file to the client.
    * Live stream extension by donation.
  * Live stream "Life of Poly"
    * Wake Poly $1.
* Own crypto and fiat acquiring.
