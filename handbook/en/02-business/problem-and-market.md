## Problem (The Problem) Polygram solves for the user

1. Diagram staticity: ordinary diagrams remain "just drawings", not connected to real processes and performers.
2. Software fragmentation: teams have to switch between 5–10 different services (Miro, Notion, Jira, No-code platforms).
3. Information overload: classic dashboards and ERP accumulate fatigue rather than clarity.

## Solution (The Solution)

1. The user assembles an understandable visual map that immediately becomes a real living system with agents and automations. Result — reduced fragmentation, increased efficiency, full automation.
