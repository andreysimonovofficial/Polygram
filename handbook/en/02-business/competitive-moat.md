# Competitive Advantage

**Language:** **English** | [Русский](../ru/02-business/competitive-moat.md)

Why existing services (Miro, Zapier, Make, custom AI platforms, n8n) cannot quickly copy Polygram and take away users?

First — if they copy Us, it benefits Us, because investors will understand that We are first, and at the same time large players are doing similar things. Second — the founder is a visionary who built this ergonomic system. If the competitors' lot is to copy, Our path is to create innovations. Also We launch real profitable AI Corporation cases under Our brands. We not only develop and maintain, We play diversification and have a full range of service and related areas.
