# Hook and UX Psychology

**Language:** **English** | [Русский](../ru/03-product-design/ux-psychology-and-hook.md)

## "Hook" section

Polygram is addictive. When you want to understand how to do something, a thought appears in your head: I need to create a Process on the canvas, specify its Request and Output and decompose it, and then you simply delegate it to a team of Agents right inside the system.

## Decomposition conditioned reflex

Every time you have a task or idea, the brain automatically thinks "I will create a Process". This is not logic — it is already a conditioned reflex. This is a real flow and soulful enjoyment.

## Delegation to personal AI Assistant

Then you need a personal AI Assistant so you can delegate. Then you communicate with the AI Assistant more than you move the mouse and press buttons, because He does it.

## White Hat Octalysis gamification

Positive stimuli should predominate (White Hat Octalysis). And payment should be made from a passionate feeling, not from limitation frames and irritation from deficit.

## Target emotional state

Euphoria and calmness! Feeling of an understandable path, seeing images and a strange desire to continue due to light cognitive load.

Polygram makes you fall in love with it and rewards you for your efforts.
