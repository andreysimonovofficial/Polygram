# Design System and References

**Language:** **English** | [Русский](../ru/03-product-design/design-system-and-refs.md)

## Aesthetic principles

Laconic, elegant, interesting, simple, convenient, smooth, fast, effective, glamorous, sexy, desirable, fresh (like clean cool morning air after a stuffy night, like pleasant hot drinking water after thirst) with principles, highly aesthetic, universal, knows its business, seamless, strict and reliable, but accessible, ergonomic and native.
