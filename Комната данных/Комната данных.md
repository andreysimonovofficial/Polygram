Below is a guide to what belongs in your data room and where each type of document goes. Having an up-to-date data room allows you to be ready for a fundraise or transaction at any moment, which is a core tenet for Forecastr customers. 

  

Corporate and Organizational Docs:

- All paperwork involved in the formation of the company
    

- Articles of incorporation
    
- LLC documentation
    
- Conversion to corporation
    
- State, Federal paperwork
    

- Current and historical Cap tables
    
- Option Grants
    
- Merger/Acquisition paperwork
    

Financing Documents:

- Loan Documents
    

- Any loan agreements and term sheets that go with them
    

- Fundraising Docs
    

- Organized by which fundraise
    

- Term sheets
    
- Board consent of fundraising
    
- Offering documentation
    

Investor Deck:

- The most up to date investor deck that you have
    

Historical Financials:

- Should have a folder for each year that the business has operated
    
- Each of these Folders should contain a monthly Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Statement of Cashflow for the entire Year
    

Financial Forecast:

- Should contain your most up to date financial model
    

People Related Docs:

- Documents from recruiting services
    
- Employees - organized by employee folder
    

- Signed Offer letter
    
- Resume
    
- Any other signed documentation (NDA, invention assignment agreement)
    

- Other People Related Docs - organized by individual person folder
    

- Contracts
    
- Any other signed documentation (NDA, invention assignment agreement)
    

Growth and Strategic Planning:

- Growth and strategic planning
    

- Any kind of written out plans for the vision of the business
    

- KPI and OKR tracking
    

- Whatever you use to track the progress of your business should be shared here
    

- Sales and Marketing Collateral
    

- Any kind of sales and marketing documentation
    
- Branding Guide
    

Customer References & Testimonials:

- Testimonials from customers
    
- NPS and survey results
    
- Documentation of customers talking about your company to others on social media
    

Competitive Landscape:

- Documentation of your competitors (can be a list with attributes listed out)
    

Product Roadmap:

- Demos of the product
    
- Screenshot of the product
    
- Roadmap document 
    

Monthly Company Updates:

- Every monthly update that you have sent out to investors or advisors
    

Other Legal & IP:

- Tax Returns
    
- Legal Docs
    
- Contracts
    
- Leases
